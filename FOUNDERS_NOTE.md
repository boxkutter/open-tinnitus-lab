# Founder’s Note

I'm a musician.

I also have tinnitus.

I'd quite like to get rid of it.

That's why I started the Open Tinnitus Lab.

I'm not a scientist, doctor, neuroscientist or tinnitus researcher. I honestly have no idea what I'm doing. I'm just a musician with tinnitus who has become frustrated by how little progress seems to be being made towards actually solving it.

There is a huge amount of tinnitus research, but it's fragmented, complicated and difficult to connect. So I'm going to see if I can build something that helps make sense of it.

And then there's AI.

I'm not a fan.

As a musician, I don't particularly like watching AI companies use human creativity to make machines that compete with musicians and artists. I don't want AI replacing people who make things.

But if we're going to have AI, let's use it for something worthwhile.

There is far too much tinnitus research for any one person to realistically process. Maybe AI can help connect it, find gaps and contradictions, and help researchers ask better questions.

Or maybe AI is completely useless.

**So this is the experiment: either I'll prove AI is useless to the human race, or we'll solve tinnitus.**

I'm hoping for the second one.

I don't claim to have the answers. I just want to see if we can find them.

— **Founder, Open Tinnitus Lab** (P.S. I wrote this with bloody AI)
